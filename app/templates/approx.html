An estimation race. Put an interval around answers nobody knows exactly: the tighter the interval the better, but miss and your score doubles.

Join a game

Get the code from whoever's hosting.

How it works

  • Every question's answer is a positive number. For each, submit an interval: a low and a high. It's good if the true answer is inside it.
  • Your score is (10 + Σ ⌊high ÷ low⌋ over good intervals) × 2missed. Every question you miss or leave blank doubles it.
  • Lowest score wins.
  • You get a few more submissions than questions (13 questions, 18 submissions). Re-submit to fix one; only your last interval for a question counts.
  • Scientific notation is fine: 3e6, 3x10^6, 10^7.
  • The game ends when the clock runs out or every team has finished.

Host a game

Pick questions from the bank or write your own, with the answer. You run the game and see the leaderboard; you don't play.
