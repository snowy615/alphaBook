Applicant guide

How to apply to Quant Bootcamp or Quant Analyst, for current University of Oxford students. Details of the outreach event are in section 3.

At a glance

  1. Sign up at alphabook.uk/signup and verify your email.
  2. Pick a programme at alphabook.uk/apply, and confirm your Oxford email if asked.
  3. Tell us if you're coming to the Quant Outreach event.
  4. Upload your CV and fill in your details.
  5. Do the {{ minutes }}-minute written assessment (not needed with Fast-Track).
  6. If shortlisted, give your availability and confirm an interview time.
  7. Get the decision by email.
1. Before you start 2. Sign up 3. Event, CV and details 4. The assessment 5. Interview 6. Decision and help

1 Before you start

  • Quant Bootcamp is the taught track, for little or no experience. Quant Analyst is the research track, for lots of experience. Fundamental uses a separate form.
  • You'll need your Oxford email, your CV as a PDF, and {{ minutes }} quiet minutes for the written assessment.

2 Sign up and choose a programme

  1. Go to alphabook.uk/apply and click Sign up to apply. Open the email we send and click the link to verify your address.
  2. Sign in, go back to the apply page and pick Quant Bootcamp or Quant Analyst.
  3. If you signed up with a non-Oxford email, enter your Oxford email. We send a 6-digit code there: type it in and click Confirm. All updates about your application go to that address.

3 Event, CV and details

First, tell us whether you're coming to the Quant Outreach event (date, time and place are on Events):

OptionWhat happens next
CV clinic + Fast-TrackYour CV round happens in person at the event: an analyst reviews your CV with you and asks you a quick question, instead of the online written assessment. After that it's the same process as everyone else, including the interview. First {{ fast_track_capacity }} places, and you can only use it once.
General attendanceCome to the event, then apply online as normal.
Not attendingApply online as normal.

Then upload your CV as a PDF (or keep the one already on your profile), fill in your first and last name, college, degree and year of study, and click Continue.

Fast-Track? You're done online at this point. We'll see you at the CV clinic and be in touch after it.

4 The written assessment

Two timed questions, one straight after the other: part one takes {{ motivation_minutes }} minutes and part two, an estimation question, takes {{ estimation_minutes }} minutes. Read the rules, tick the box and click Start the assessment when you're ready.

  • One attempt, no pausing. The timers keep running even if you close the tab.
  • Stay in full screen until you finish. Time out of full screen is recorded.
  • No pasting (it's blocked), and no copying the questions. Leaving the page or switching tabs is recorded.
  • No AI and no outside help.
  • Your answers save automatically, and each part submits itself at 0:00. Click Submit to move on early.
  • For maths, type LaTeX between dollar signs (for example $x^2$) and click Preview.

5 Interview

If you're shortlisted, we'll email you to ask for your availability. On the apply page, click every 30-minute slot you could do (London time). It saves automatically.

An analyst picks one of your slots and schedules the interview; there's nothing to confirm. You'll get an email with the time, the Google Meet link, your interviewer's contact and a calendar invite. If something comes up, contact your interviewer directly. Interviews take about {{ interview_minutes }} minutes.

6 Decision and help

You'll get the decision by email. If you're accepted, your membership updates automatically. If not, Bootcamp applicants can reapply next year, or apply as an Analyst next term with Apply again.
