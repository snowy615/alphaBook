Frequently asked questions

About applying to Alpha Fund, the assessment, and what happens after.

Applying

Who can apply?

Any current University of Oxford student. General public applicants confirm their student status as part of applying; anyone already an Alpha Fund member has already cleared that bar.

What tracks are there, and can I apply to more than one?

Two subjects — Fundamental and Quant — each with a Bootcamp (taught) and an Analyst (research) tier. There's no combined "apply to both" option: pick one track to apply to now, and once that application is decided you're welcome to apply to the other. A Bootcamp member can also apply on to the matching Analyst tier without reapplying from scratch.

Is Fundamental open yet?

Not yet — Fundamental Bootcamp and Fundamental Analyst are shown on the apply page as coming next term. Quant is open now.

Do I need a CV?

Yes — an up-to-date CV goes on your profile as part of applying, and it's what reviewers read alongside your written answers.

The assessment

What does the assessment involve?

One 15-minute sitting, once you start it: a 5-minute question on why you want to join and why you, followed by a 10-minute estimation question (pick something large and hard to count exactly, and reason your way to a number). You can write plain text or light LaTeX for any maths.

Can I pause and come back later?

No — once you start, the clock runs to the end in one sitting. Start it only when you have 15 minutes free and won't be interrupted.

Can I use AI tools, or notes prepared in advance?

No — the assessment is meant to be your own thinking, in the moment. Pasting in text and leaving the page are both tracked, and it's the same sitting every applicant gets.

Is there still a timed numerical/quant round?

No — the assessment is the two written questions above for every track, Quant included. Quant Analyst applicants can expect the harder mathematical content to come up at interview instead.

After you submit

What happens after I submit?

The committee reviews your CV and written answers. If you're shortlisted, you'll be asked to submit your availability, then an analyst proposes an interview time for you to confirm.

How does interview scheduling work?

Once shortlisted, fill in the hours you're free on the availability grid. An analyst proposes a specific time from that — confirm it, or say it doesn't work and the interviewer will reach out directly to find another. A confirmed interview gets a calendar invite (with a Google Meet link, where available) by email.

Can I apply again if I'm rejected, or after Bootcamp?

Yes — once a decision is made, you can start a fresh application. That's also how a Bootcamp member moves on to Analyst.

Quant Outreach & Fast-Track

What is Quant Outreach?

A talk from senior Alpha Fund members about the fund, careers in quant finance, and how to get involved — see Events for the date, time and location.

What's the CV clinic + Fast-Track ticket?

Sign up for it and an analyst reviews your CV in person at the event, then you skip the online written assessment and go straight into the interview process. It's limited to the first 50 places — general attendance (no cap) is always available if Fast-Track is full, and you can still apply online afterwards either way.
