Frequently asked questions

About applying to Alpha Fund, the assessment, and what happens after.

Applying

Who can apply?

Any current University of Oxford student. General public applicants confirm their student status as part of applying; anyone already an Alpha Fund member has already cleared that bar.

What tracks are there, and can I apply to more than one?

There are two tracks, Fundamental and Quant, and each has a Bootcamp (taught) and an Analyst (research) tier. You're welcome to apply to both: Fundamental through its separate form, and Quant here on AlphaBook. If you have little or no experience, we'd recommend applying to the Bootcamp; if you already have a lot of experience, apply to join directly as an Analyst.

How do I apply for Fundamental?

Fundamental Bootcamp and Fundamental Analyst applications use a separate form, which isn't currently open on this platform. Quant is open here now.

Do I need a CV?

Yes. An up-to-date CV goes on your profile as part of applying, and it's what reviewers read alongside your written answers.

The assessment

What does the assessment involve?

Two questions, one straight after the other, each with its own timer. The first gives you 5 minutes on something you've pursued seriously and what you'd bring to the fund. The second gives you 3 minutes on an estimation question: pick something large and hard to count exactly, and reason your way to a number. You can write plain text or light LaTeX for any maths.

Can I pause and come back later?

No. Once you start, each question's timer keeps running whether or not the page is open, and the second question starts as soon as the first one ends. Start only when you have 8 minutes free and won't be interrupted.

Can I use AI tools, or notes prepared in advance?

No. The assessment is meant to be your own thinking, in the moment. Pasting in text and leaving the page are both tracked, and it's the same sitting every applicant gets.

Is there still a timed numerical/quant round?

No. The assessment is the two written questions above for every track, Quant included. Quant Analyst applicants can expect the harder mathematical content to come up at interview instead.

After you submit

What happens after I submit?

The committee reviews your CV and written answers. If you're shortlisted, you'll be asked to submit your availability, then an analyst proposes an interview time for you to confirm.

How does interview scheduling work?

Once shortlisted, fill in the availability grid. Each box is a 30-minute interview slot, so click every one you could make. An analyst proposes one of those times, and you can confirm it or say it doesn't work, in which case the interviewer will reach out directly to find another. A confirmed interview gets a calendar invite (with a Google Meet link, where available) by email.

Can I apply again if I'm rejected, or after Bootcamp?

Yes. If you're rejected from Bootcamp, you may reapply next year, or apply as an Analyst next term. A Bootcamp member moves on to Analyst the same way, by starting a fresh application.

Quant Outreach & Fast-Track

What is Quant Outreach?

A talk from senior Alpha Fund members about the fund, careers in quant finance, and how to get involved. See Events for the date, time and location.

What's the CV clinic + Fast-Track ticket?

Fast-Track means your CV round happens in person at the event instead of online: an analyst reviews your CV with you and asks you a quick question, in place of the online motivation and estimation questions. After that it's the same process as everyone else, including the interview. It's limited to the first 50 places. General attendance has no cap, so it's always available if Fast-Track is full, and you can still apply online afterwards either way.
