Lone Bid

Seven players, cards 1 to 8, eighteen rounds. Each round the lowest number that nobody else played wins. Show two cards, see everyone else's, then play one. Only the top scorer survives each checkpoint, and three are left for the final.

How it works

1 · Reveal two

Everyone starts with cards 1 to 8. At the start of a round you secretly pick two cards from your hand ({{ reveal_seconds }} seconds), and every pair is shown at once.

2 · Play one

Having seen everyone's pairs, you secretly play one of your two ({{ play_seconds }} seconds).

3 · Lowest lone number wins

Every number played more than once cancels out. The lowest number left wins: its player scores points equal to the card (a 4 scores 4) and one token. If every number is matched, nobody wins the round.

Example. Seven cards are played: 1, 1, 2, 3, 3, 5, 7. The two 1s cancel and the two 3s cancel. The lone numbers left are 2, 5 and 7, so the 2 wins: its player scores 2 points and a token. The 5 and 7 stood alone but weren't the lowest.

The catch

The card you play is gone for the rest of the set. The card you showed but kept is locked: you can't reveal it in the next round. Everyone can see what's left in every hand.

Checkpoints and resets

After rounds 3, 6, 9 and 12 The top scorer survives and leaves the table. Everyone else goes back to zero points but keeps their tokens. A tie on points goes to whoever has more tokens.
After rounds 6 and 12 Everyone gets their full hand of 1 to 8 back.
Rounds 13–{{ rounds }}: the final Three players are left. At the end, the top scorer wins the prize, second survives, and third is eliminated.

House readings

A tie on points and tokens is settled by a random draw. Locked cards are cleared when hands reset. If you don't choose before the clock runs out, you get a random legal choice. Once every person at the table has survived, the bots play the remaining rounds out straight away.

The leaderboard

Winning the prize counts 3, surviving 2, being eliminated 0. Your rating also accounts for the strength of the bots at the table.
