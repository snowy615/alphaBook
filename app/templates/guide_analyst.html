Analyst guide

Scoring candidates, running interviews and recording decisions. For Quant and Fundamental Analysts, and admins. Only analysts and admins can see this page.

At a glance

  1. Get analyst access on your Profile.
  2. Open the review page at alphabook.uk/apply/admin.
  3. Score each CV round (out of {{ cv_max }}), or use Automatic shortlist.
  4. Shortlist or reject, then propose an interview time from their availability.
  5. Run the interview with the built-in timer and score it (out of {{ interview_max }}).
  6. Accept or reject, after agreeing it with the committee.

1 Get access

  1. Sign up at alphabook.uk/signup and verify your email.
  2. On Profile, set Membership to Quant Analyst (or Fundamental Analyst), enter the analyst password from the committee, and click Save.
  3. Go to alphabook.uk/apply/admin.

How scoring works. Any number of analysts can score a candidate, and the page shows the average. Everything saves as you go and stays editable. Candidates never see scores or comments.

2 The review page

  • The tabs filter by stage. Fast-Track holds Fast-Track applicants waiting for their in-person CV round; My pending interviews shows interviews assigned to you.
  • The list is ranked by CV score. The Shortlisted tab ranks by the average of CV and interview, each put on a 10-point scale.
  • All unreviewed shows candidates nobody has scored yet.
  • Scoring guide has the full rubrics and interview rules; Export to Excel downloads everything.

3 Score the CV round

On a candidate's card, click Score CV and answers (online applicants) or Score onsite CV (Fast-Track, scored at the CV clinic). Pick one option per criterion; the total counts once every criterion is picked. Both rubrics are out of {{ cv_max }}:

What each score looks like is in the Scoring guide, and next to every criterion in the scoring view.

  • Use the descriptions as a guide. When in doubt, pick the score that best fits your overall impression.
  • Be consistent across candidates, and judge the depth and quality of evidence, not the quantity.
  • Every criterion applies to every applicant, including first years.
  • For finance internships or trading experience, note the firm, role, duration and responsibilities so they can be checked at interview.
  • We aim for Year 1 applicants to make up at least 50% of the interview cohort (exact share to be confirmed).
  • Use General comments for notes (one box per candidate, shared across rounds). Click Close or press Esc when done; it's already saved.

Automatic shortlist. A candidate who meets any one of these goes straight to interview with no CV score: click Automatic shortlist on their card and choose the criterion.

4 Shortlist and schedule

At the bottom of the card, click Shortlist for interview or Reject (add a note if useful). The candidate is emailed automatically and asked for their availability.

  1. Click a green slot; the date and time fill in.
  2. Choose the interviewer, add an optional message and click Schedule interview.

That's it: nobody has to confirm. The candidate and the interviewer get one email together with the time, the Google Meet link, each other's contact and a calendar invite. If the time needs to change, they sort it out directly, and you can pick a new slot the same way (Move to this time), which emails both of them again.

6 Decide

  • Click Accept or Reject on the card. Accept grants membership immediately, so agree it with the committee first.
  • Admins also have Send reminder, Email applicants (one message to any group you choose), Let them redo the assessment and Delete application.
  • Score independently, follow the rubric and timings, and keep everything confidential.
