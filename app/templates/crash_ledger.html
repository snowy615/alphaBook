How to read the numbers

Every card below pulls from the same nine metrics. Here's what each one means before you dig into the tickers.

Periods with data
How many of the 6 defined crash periods this stock has price history for.
Observations
Total daily price readings used across those crash periods.
Avg. crash return
The mean % price change across all crash periods the stock was present for.
Median crash return
The middle-ranked % return across periods — less skewed by one extreme outlier than the average.
Best / worst period
The single best-performing and worst-performing crash period on record for this stock.
Worst max drawdown
The deepest peak-to-trough decline recorded in any single crash period — the most pain a holder would have felt.
Avg. daily volatility
The average size of day-to-day price swings during crash periods — higher means choppier, less predictable price action.
Total return, all periods
The compounded return if all of that stock's crash periods are strung together back to back.
