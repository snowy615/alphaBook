CV round guidance

  • Every criterion applies to every applicant, first years included.
  • We aim for Year 1 applicants to make up at least 50% of the interview cohort (exact share to be confirmed).
  • Use the descriptions as a guide. When in doubt, pick the score that best fits your overall impression.
  • Focus on the depth and quality of the evidence, not the quantity. Be consistent across candidates.
  • Relevant technical projects include research with code, data analysis, app or software development, modelling, ML / AI projects, and other substantial coding work.
  • For finance internships or trading experience, note the key details in your comments so they can be checked at interview.

Key rules for interviewers

  • Two questions, 10 minutes each. Give the candidate 10 minutes for the easy question and 10 minutes for the hard one, timed from when each has been read out and any clarifying questions answered. At 10 minutes, stop the question and move on. The Interview tab of the scoring view has a timer (Start CV, Start easy, Start hard) that calls out each step.
  • Everyone attempts both questions, whatever happened on the easy one.
  • Hints only on the schedule, and checkpoints decide whether one is needed. Each question in the bank lists Hint 1, Hint 2, and the checkpoint each hint gets the candidate to. At 3:00, give Hint 1 only if they haven't reached Checkpoint 1; at 6:00, give Hint 2 only if they haven't reached Checkpoint 2. That's the whole test: not how confident they seem, or whether they ask.
  • No early hints, and only the official wording. If a candidate asks before a hint is due, tell them the next one comes at its time if they still need it. Give each hint at the next natural pause after its time, and read it as written in the question bank.
  • Use the score criteria. Score on the candidate's progress and reasoning within the time limit. Don't give extra time to let them finish.
  • Clarification is allowed. Answering questions about wording or assumptions is fine and doesn't affect the score. Beyond that, no help other than the scheduled hints.
  • Likability: 2 should be the typical score. Keep 3 for someone you'd particularly want on the team.
  • Be consistent. Follow the same structure, timing and level of guidance for every candidate.
  • Overall impression. Use the descriptions as guidelines, alongside your overall impression of the interview.
